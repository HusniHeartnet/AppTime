Share with the AppTime community?

AppTime can send anonymous daily totals of which apps were on your screen, so the community can see which apps people use most. Combined results (never anyone's own numbers) are posted in the AppTime community and on social media. It's completely optional, and AppTime works the same either way.

What's shared, once a day

  • Your total screen time for the day
  • Each app's name and how long it was on screen
  • A random ID that isn't linked to you

Never shared

  • Window titles, websites, files or anything you type
  • Your name, account or computer name

Only days from today onwards are shared. You can see exactly what's sent, hide apps, turn it off, or delete your shared data any time from Community sharing at the bottom of this page. Privacy policy
