# AppTime privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

## The short version

AppTime keeps your screen time on your own computer. Nothing leaves your computer
unless you turn on **community sharing**. If you do, AppTime sends anonymous daily
totals of how long each app was on your screen. We combine everyone's totals to
see which apps our community uses most, and post the combined results. You can
stop sharing and delete your shared data at any time.

## Who runs this

AppTime community sharing is run by **Husni Heartnet**.
Contact: **husniheartnet** on Discord.

## What is sent (only if you turn sharing on)

- A random install ID, created on your computer. It isn't linked to your name, account or device.
- For each finished day, starting from the day you turned sharing on:
  - the date
  - your total screen time
  - for each app: its program file name (like `chrome.exe`), its display name (like "Google Chrome") and how many seconds it was on screen
- The AppTime version.

You can see exactly what will be sent in AppTime under **Community sharing**, and
you can hide any app from sharing.

## What is never sent

- Window titles, website addresses, file names or anything you type
- Your name, email, username or computer name
- Hour-by-hour timing (only daily totals)

Our server sees your IP address, as with any internet connection. It is only used to
limit abuse (too many uploads in an hour). It is kept in memory for at most an hour,
and is never stored or logged.

## Why, and what we publish

We use the data to make community statistics: which apps the most people use, total
hours, average screen time and a "Hivemind score" (how similar everyone's top apps are).
We post these **combined** results in our Discord and on social media for fun and discussion.

- Individual data is never published.
- An app is only included once at least 3 people use it, so an unusual app can't point to one person.

The legal basis is your consent, which you give by turning sharing on.

## How long it's kept

Until you delete it, or **2 years** after the day it describes, whichever comes first.

## Your choices

- **Stop sharing:** turn sharing off in AppTime. Nothing more is sent.
- **Delete your data:** press **Delete my shared data** in AppTime. Everything sent under
  your install ID is deleted from our server immediately.
- If you uninstalled AppTime before deleting, contact us. Without your install ID we can't
  tell which data is yours, which is also why we can't identify you.

## Where it's stored

On a server we rent from **Biznet Gio** in **Indonesia**. Data is sent over an
encrypted connection. We don't sell or share the raw data with anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what's collected, AppTime will ask for
your agreement again.
